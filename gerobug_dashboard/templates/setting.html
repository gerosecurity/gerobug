Resend Email

If an email failed to reach the hunter (e.g. a bounced delivery), resend the last email an operator initiated for a report — invalid notices, amend/update requests, bounty calculations, NDA/prerequisite requests, status notifications, and completion emails (certificate). Automated replies to the hunter (invalid format, unauthorized, unknown report ID) are never resent. The original message text, reasons, and attachments are re-sent as-is; no status, score, or permission changes are made. Resending a bounty calculation while the report is still at Bounty Calculation restarts the hunter's 30-day appeal window from the resend. Enter the Report ID.

Hunter Email Sinkhole

While active, every outgoing email to bug hunters (status notifications, NDA/bounty/completion emails, auto-replies) is silently dropped — nothing is sent, but report status/permission updates still happen normally. Use this before manually re-requesting an update or marking a hunter email unread for re-ingestion, to avoid double-notifying them. Admin password-reset email and Slack/Telegram notifications are not affected. This does not auto-disable — turn it off here when you're done.
