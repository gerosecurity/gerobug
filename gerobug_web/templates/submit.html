What happens next

  1. Received. We received your report and it is waiting for a triager.
  2. In Review. A triager is reviewing your report and may ask you for more information.
  3. Confirmed. The issue is confirmed and is being fixed. We may ask you to retest.
  4. Retest. A fix is in place and is being retested.
  5. Bounty Calculation. We sent you a bounty decision. Agree or appeal before the deadline.
  6. Bounty in Process. You agreed to the decision. We may ask for documents before payment.
  7. Completed. This report is complete. Thank you for your contribution.
